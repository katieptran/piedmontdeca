Common questions

Before you get started

Who can join Piedmont DECA?

Students enrolled in AP Business and Personal Finance can join. Contact Mr. Christensen for registration details.

Where can I find meeting dates?

Chapter meetings take place every Tuesday during lunch in Room 204.

How can families and local businesses help?

Our sponsorship options range from Friend of DECA to Platinum Diamond. View the contribution levels and benefits, then contact Tobias Tsai or Mr. Christensen.

Who should I contact with questions?

Email our advisor, Mr. Christensen, at fchristensen@piedmont.k12.ca.us. You can also connect with the officer team through the chapter’s Instagram.
