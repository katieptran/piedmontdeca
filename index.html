Membership information

Joining Piedmont DECA

To join Piedmont DECA, you must be enrolled in AP Business and Personal Finance.

  1. Contact our chapter advisor, Mr. Christensen, to learn how to join.
  2. Confirm your enrollment in AP Business and Personal Finance, then ask about the next meeting and registration process.
  3. Follow the chapter on Instagram for updates and announcements.

The chapter's official signup link and contact details are awaiting confirmation.
